# App Store Marketing Plan

## Suggested GitHub Issue Title

Marketing plan for Apple App Store and Google Play launch

## Summary

Real Fake Birds should launch as a curiosity-first daily trivia game, not as a niche birding utility. The acquisition hook is immediate and legible: guess whether a bird name is real or fake. The retention hook is already in the product: daily challenge, streaks, missions, badges, themes, journal, birdhouse collection, leaderboard, and optional account sync.

This plan assumes the app stores are the primary conversion surface, the web build remains the pre-launch proving ground, and the work must stay realistic for a solo developer over the next few weeks.

## Objectives

- Turn existing web traffic and testers into launch-day installs
- Build store pages that clearly explain the hook in the first screenful
- Use low-maintenance channels with durable upside instead of high-volume social posting
- Support launch-week installs with better share prompts, review timing, and lightweight outreach

## Success Metrics

- Waitlist or notify-me conversion from web visitors
- Store page view-to-install conversion
- Install-to-first-round completion rate
- Daily challenge starts and completions
- Share rate after milestones
- Launch-week ratings volume and average rating
- Day 1 and Day 7 retention from launch cohorts

## Constraints

- Solo developer bandwidth
- App-store launch in the next few weeks
- No dependence on daily social media maintenance
- Marketing should not outrun product readiness, packaging, or QA stability

## Marketing Thesis

Lead with the simplest promise: guess which bird names are real. Keep the brand line as the closer: Some are real. Some are fake. Tell them apart.

The game can reach beyond birders if the messaging stays playful and legible. The launch should target casual trivia players, curious nature players, and collection-driven mobile players before leaning into more specialized birding communities.

## Core Audiences

### Casual Trivia Players

- Angle: fast rounds, funny names, daily habit, streak pressure
- Best channels: app-store keywords, Apple Search Ads, gaming communities, playable web funnel

### Nature-Curious Players

- Angle: weird real bird names are surprising even if you know nothing about birds
- Best channels: short gameplay clips, store screenshots, lightweight newsletter outreach

### Birding and Wildlife Players

- Angle: prove you know your birds
- Best channels: one-time launch posts to birding-adjacent communities after polish and factual quality are stable

### Collection and Progression Players

- Angle: seeds, badges, themes, journal, birdhouse, leaderboard
- Best channels: store screenshots, milestone sharing, onboarding and reveal moments

## Plan By Phase

### Phase 1: Now Until Store Submission

- Turn the web build into a launch funnel with a clear iPhone and Android notify-me CTA
- Finalize launch positioning, keyword targets, and store copy direction before asset production
- Record short mobile-first gameplay clips from a near-final build
- Recruit a small beta cohort from existing web players and personal network
- Build one dashboard that ties creative and channel performance to first-session behavior
- Prepare a lightweight press kit and a short list of launch targets for one-time outreach

### Phase 2: Launch Week

- Replace notify-me CTA with live store badges on the site
- Send one launch email to the waitlist with direct links and one strong gameplay clip
- Post once to a small set of high-fit communities instead of spreading effort across many channels
- Run narrow Apple Search Ads exact-match campaigns and keep spend constrained until store conversion is proven
- Monitor launch-day funnel metrics and update screenshot order or copy only if the data supports it

### Phase 3: First 30 Days

- Double down only on channels that produce real round completion and return behavior
- Test one or two store metadata changes rather than introducing major new channels
- Tighten review prompts and share prompts around positive moments
- Ship one small retention-supporting beat, such as launch-month badge framing or featured challenge messaging, without creating permanent live-ops burden

## Channel Strategy

### Primary

- App Store Optimization for Apple and Google
- Web build as a playable pre-launch funnel
- In-product sharing around streaks, badges, and daily challenge moments
- Closed beta testers as review and feedback seed cohort

### Secondary

- Apple Search Ads with exact-intent keywords
- One small paid creative test to the web build before launch
- One-time outreach to targeted communities and newsletters

### Avoid For This Launch

- Daily social posting requirements
- Heavy influencer outreach programs
- Broad paid acquisition before crash-free sessions and store conversion are stable

## Store Messaging Direction

### Title Concepts

- Real Fake Birds
- Real Fake Birds: Bird Quiz
- Real Fake Birds: Trivia Game

### Subtitle or Short Description Concepts

- Spot the real bird names
- Guess which bird names are fake
- Weird bird names. Real quiz.

### Screenshot Sequence

1. Show the core choice immediately: a bird name and real-or-fake decision
2. Show the reveal payoff with image or fact content
3. Show progression: streaks, seeds, badges, theme unlocks
4. Show daily challenge and daily missions
5. Show journal and birdhouse collection depth
6. Show leaderboard and optional account sync

### Video Direction

Keep the trailer under 15 seconds. The first three seconds should show the core choice clearly with sound off. End on the brand line.

## Review Strategy

- Prompt only after positive moments such as a completed daily challenge, badge unlock, or meaningful streak
- Never prompt in the first session or after an error state
- Use beta testers and waitlist players for honest launch-week reviews
- Route negative sentiment to feedback before sending users to a store review prompt

## Dependencies

- Final screenshot and video capture depend on a near-final mobile build
- Share prompts depend on stable sharing payloads and launch-ready copy
- Review prompts depend on core progression and sync flows feeling reliable
- Paid tests depend on analytics being ready to measure first-session quality

## Child Issue Backlog

### 1. Finalize Launch Positioning And Keyword Set

- Why it matters: the launch copy, screenshots, and ads will drift without one approved positioning sheet
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: S
- Acceptance criteria:
- One positioning document exists with primary hook, three audience angles, and fifteen target keywords
- The approved tagline, store tone, and launch copy direction are defined

### 2. Add Notify-Me CTA To The Web Build

- Why it matters: converts existing traffic into launch-day demand instead of wasting it
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- The homepage includes iPhone and Android launch reminder messaging
- Notify-me submission is tracked in analytics
- Submission flow works on mobile and desktop

### 3. Build A Launch Funnel Analytics Dashboard

- Why it matters: channel and creative decisions should be tied to actual play behavior
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Dashboard tracks landing visits, notify-me submissions, store clicks, first-round completion, daily challenge starts, share events, and repeat sessions
- Metrics are visible in one place before launch week

### 4. Produce Final Store Screenshot Sets

- Why it matters: the store pages are the main conversion surface for a solo launch
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Six launch screenshots are exported for Apple and Google in correct formats
- Captions are finalized and consistent with store positioning

### 5. Cut Three Launch Gameplay Creatives

- Why it matters: short clips are needed for launch posts, creative testing, and store-adjacent use
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Three distinct vertical gameplay clips under fifteen seconds exist
- Each clip uses a different hook and works with sound off

### 6. Create A Lightweight Press Kit Page

- Why it matters: makes one-time outreach credible and fast to execute
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: S
- Acceptance criteria:
- Press kit includes logo, screenshots, short description, founder bio, contact, and launch links placeholder
- Link can be shared externally without follow-up explanation

### 7. Recruit A Focused Beta And Review Cohort

- Why it matters: early testers can surface issues and seed honest launch-week reviews
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Twenty to fifty invited testers receive access
- A feedback collection path exists
- At least ten meaningful responses are logged before public launch

### 8. Prepare Launch-Week Outreach List

- Why it matters: avoids scrambling on launch day and keeps outreach high-fit
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: S
- Acceptance criteria:
- A list of ten to twenty launch targets exists with URLs, angle, status, and target day
- Launch copy for each major target type is drafted

### 9. Improve Share Prompts Around Positive Milestones

- Why it matters: organic sharing is more likely to work than broad top-of-funnel posting for this game
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Share prompts are tied to defined milestone moments
- Copy variants exist for streak, badge, and daily challenge outcomes
- Share usage is instrumented for measurement

### 10. Configure Apple Search Ads For Exact-Intent Keywords

- Why it matters: best-fit paid channel for a small iOS launch budget
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: S
- Acceptance criteria:
- Campaign structure, keyword list, negatives, budget cap, and launch timing are defined
- Install tracking plan is documented before spend begins

### 11. Gate Review Prompts To Positive Moments Only

- Why it matters: launch-week ratings will affect store conversion immediately
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: M
- Acceptance criteria:
- Review prompt logic is defined for milestone moments only
- Prompt is suppressed after errors, early sessions, or unstable flows

### 12. Prepare Launch-Day Website And Email Switch-Over

- Why it matters: launch-day messaging should change cleanly without last-minute edits
- Owner: solo dev
- Effort: S
- Acceptance criteria:
- Site CTA, waitlist email, store badges, and launch copy are ready to publish in one pass

## Definition Of Done

- Store positioning, assets, and outreach plan are ready before submission approval
- Notify-me funnel and launch analytics are in place before launch week
- Launch-week tactics are constrained to channels the solo developer can actually operate
- Marketing does not create new blocking dependencies for packaging, QA, or compliance work
